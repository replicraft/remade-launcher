# Asset provenance and rights

## Existing Remade originals

The two source SVGs were recovered from the user's saved Remade project files through their exact text content. The saved files were named `remade-mark.svg` and `favicon(2).svg`; the latter is normalized here to `remade-favicon.svg`. Raw-byte materialization was unavailable, so the recorded SVG text was reconstructed with its terminal newline. The resulting lengths are 264 and 484 bytes, matching the saved-file metadata. No live-site favicon substitution or invented source URL is claimed.

The transparent mark defines an outlined square and a filled overlapping square with an even-odd cutout. The favicon adds a `#15191F` rounded-square ground and uses `#C4F477` for the mark. Both exact source texts remain in `source/` for inspection. These are the existing Remade identity, not a newly generated logo.

## Derivations

`master/remade-launcher.svg` preserves the favicon source. The macOS master adds a transparent outer canvas and transforms the same tile into a centered 824-by-824 pixel region of a 1024-pixel canvas. Monochrome tray marks mechanically replace the lime fill/stroke with black or white. Raster sizes and container representations are platform exports, not separate creative identities.

The contact sheet renders labels with an environment font solely into a preview image. No font file is included. The preview is not application UI or a product screenshot and should not be used as a fictional screenshot on the download page.

## Rights and use

The user supplied the Remade identity through prior project artifacts. This pack does not assign a new license or waive brand/trademark ownership. Confirm the repository's asset policy and public distribution terms with the owner before release. Do not apply a dependency's license to these brand files by association.

The platform icon family is separate from future application-card fallback artwork and platform-symbol glyphs. Those UI glyphs require their own original design or reviewed licensed source during implementation. No external design-reference assets are included in this bundle.

## Evidence limits

The pack validates dimensions, container structure, image decoding, hashes, and a visual contact sheet. It does not claim native Finder, Explorer, installer, Dock, icon-cache, or screen-reader tests. Those remain release evidence obligations in the platform chapters.
